# Disclaimer and Intended Use

Rehab Candidate Review is an educational organization and decision-support prototype.

It may summarize supporting factors, concerns, missing information, suggested follow-up questions, medical or functional barriers, facility-specific capability issues, and a preliminary organizational score.

It does not make or replace:

- Admission or denial decisions
- Treatment recommendations
- Medical-necessity or coverage determinations
- Rehabilitation-physician review
- Nursing, therapy, or case-management review
- Receiving-facility or treating-specialist review
- Facility-policy or payer review
- Professional clinical judgment

The scoring model and decision rules have not been clinically validated.

Some thresholds and operational requirements reflect feedback about one facility’s capabilities. They must not be treated as universal medical, regulatory, Medicare, payer, or rehabilitation-admission criteria.

Questions involving chemotherapy, radiation, dialysis, oxygen support, cardiac status, laboratory abnormalities, diabetes treatment, IV infusions, or other medical conditions require review by the appropriate treating clinicians and receiving facility.

## Privacy

Do not enter names, dates of birth, medical-record numbers, room numbers, addresses, exact dates, copied chart notes, or other identifying information.

Use generic labels such as `Patient 1`.

The prototype has no backend, database, accounts, analytics, chart uploads, or intentional patient-record storage.

Use only fictional cases during initial testing.
