# Disclaimer and Intended Use

Rehab Candidate Review is an educational decision-support prototype.

It may summarize supporting factors, concerns, missing information, suggested follow-up questions, and a preliminary support score.

It does not make or replace:

- Admission decisions
- Denial decisions
- Medical-necessity determinations
- Coverage determinations
- Physician review
- Facility-policy review
- Payer review
- Professional clinical judgment

The scoring model has not been clinically validated.

## Privacy

Do not enter names, dates of birth, medical-record numbers, room numbers, addresses, exact dates, copied chart notes, or other identifying information.

Use generic labels such as `Patient 1`.

The prototype has no backend, database, accounts, analytics, chart uploads, or intentional patient-record storage.

It should initially be tested only with fictional cases.
